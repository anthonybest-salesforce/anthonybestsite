Design lineage
Tiger Woods won 13 of his 14 majors with a Newport-style Cameron. The Special Select represents the apex of that tradition for a new era — insert-free 303 stainless, uncompromised, with cherry rings that echo legendary tournament-winning models.
Why the solid milled face is a master class in feedback
Without an insert, every putt speaks directly through 303 stainless steel to your hands. Center strikes feel smooth and fast. Off-center contact sounds and feels immediately different — slightly hollow, with a telltale distance loss. This unfiltered communication trains a putting stroke at a neurological level. You have not simply learned to putt with this putter. You have been educated by it.
Why the revamped plumbing neck has shaped your stroke arc
The Special Select's revamped plumbing neck creates mid toe hang — the face drops to roughly 45° when balanced, with freer visual access to the flatter, narrower topline at address. This naturally encourages the face to open slightly on the backswing and close through impact. Several years of putting with this geometry means your motor pattern is built around exactly this toe-flow behavior. It is a deeply ingrained competitive asset.
Why the fixed tungsten sole weights are a deliberate advantage
The Special Select's fixed 35g tungsten sole weights at heel and toe are a purposeful engineering decision over adjustable screws — maximum perimeter mass placed where it matters most. This gives a blade-format putter more MOI than any equivalent steel-only design. It is the reason this Newport performs beyond what its profile suggests.
"The Special Select Newport evolves a legendary silhouette into its most performance-complete form — the classic blade shape, now engineered with Tour-level precision and tungsten stability." — Scotty Cameron, on the Special Select Newport design brief
Why now? The Special Select has not failed. It has done its job so well that the stroke it trained is now refined enough to benefit from a higher-ceiling instrument. This is not a replacement. It is a graduation.

10 · Risk Register
Every objection,

Risk: High · Probability: Low
Visual adjustment at address
All three finalists are wider than the Special Select Newport blade. A small percentage of blade players experience brief hesitation before the larger mallet head becomes second nature.
Mitigation15-minute green-side acclimation before competitive use. The fitting session itself resolves this — the right finalist will feel immediately natural. Most players report comfort within a single practice session.
Risk: Medium · Probability: Low
Feel recalibration from solid to insert
The Special Select Newport's solid face delivers raw stainless feedback. All three finalists use the Carbon Steel chain-link insert, which is softer. Some players initially misread the difference as reduced feedback.
MitigationWithin 3–5 rounds, the new feel becomes the reference point. The insert provides equivalent distance information through a different sensory register — softer sound, same precision. One fitting session eliminates the adjustment period entirely.
Risk: Low · Probability: Low
Initial tempo recalibration
Higher MOI slows face rotation slightly. Some players over-rotate in the first round or two as the stroke compensates for inertia it no longer needs to overcome.
MitigationOne SAM PuttLab or Capto session — 90 minutes — eliminates this entirely. The data-backed recalibration is faster than the adjustment curve of any other equipment upgrade in the bag.
