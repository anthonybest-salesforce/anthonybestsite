07 · MOI Analysis
The forgiveness numbers,
calculated.
What MOI measures
Moment of Inertia (g·cm²) is a putter head's resistance to twisting on off-center contact. Governed by I = Σ m·r² — the sum of each mass element multiplied by the square of its distance from the center of gravity. Higher MOI = less face twist = less distance and directional loss on imperfect contact.
How designers maximize it
Because MOI scales with r², doubling the distance of a mass from the center quadruples its MOI contribution. This is why wings, flanges, and perimeter weighting are so effective — they move mass furthest from the sweet spot, where the r² multiplier is highest.

09 · Neck Architecture
The plumbing neck is
a non-negotiable match.
Why neck architecture matters
The hosel-to-shaft connection governs where the shaft sits relative to the face's center of mass, directly setting toe hang and face balance. A neck mismatch forces the player to manipulate the putter face through impact — the single most common root cause of directional inconsistency in skilled players.
Your current neck: Plumbing neck
The plumbing neck routes the shaft directly through the top of the hosel in a near-vertical line — placing it over the putter's sweet spot. This is the Scotty Cameron hallmark geometry and is not replicated precisely by any other brand's proprietary neck systems. Switching brands means switching neck geometry.
Plumbing Neck ✓
Shaft runs straight through hosel top. Near-zero offset. Low toe hang. Direct continuation of your current geometry — all three finalists use this exact architecture.
Slant Neck ✗
Hosel angles toward the shaft, creating moderate offset and medium toe hang. Requires a slight arc stroke — a mismatch for your profile. Eliminated in round one.
Flow Neck ✗
Full-shaft offset. Higher toe hang. Best for strong-arc strokes — the opposite of your technique. Eliminated in round one.
Short Slant ✗
Compact hosel with light offset. Variable face balance across models. Inconsistent fit profile — eliminated alongside slant-neck models.
